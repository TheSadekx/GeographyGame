# 🌍 GeographyGame
## Geography Q&A Game: Tectonic and Volcanic Movements

This project is an interactive Q&A game developed based on the **gamification** model in geography education. It aims to help students and geography enthusiasts learn challenging topics such as crustal movements, fault lines, earthquakes, and volcanism in a fun and permanent way.

---

## 🎯 Purpose and Benefits of the Project

We aimed to bring tectonic and volcanic processes, which often remain abstract or difficult to memorize in traditional geography lessons, into a dynamic digital environment.

* **Gamified Learning:** It steps outside traditional rote-learning methods, allowing users to test their knowledge with instant feedback.
* **Visual and Permanent Memory:** Thanks to the reasoning processes triggered by the questions, the structure of fault lines and the logic of volcanic activities become more permanent in mind.
* **Academic Support:** It is an excellent interactive revision tool for students preparing for school exams or standardized tests.
* **Accessible Education:** It presents complex geological processes in a simple, clear, and entertaining language that everyone can understand.

---

## 🌋 Game Content and Scope

The game features carefully selected question pools covering the following main themes:
* **Tectonic Movements:** Plate tectonics, zones of plate convergence, and divergence.
* **Fault Lines and Earthquakes:** Reverse, strike-slip, and normal faults; seismic waves, and regional fault systems.
* **Volcanism:** Intrusive and extrusive volcanism, volcanic landforms (crater, caldera, maar), and active volcanic regions.
